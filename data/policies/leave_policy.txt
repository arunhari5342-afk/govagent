# Employee Leave Policy

## Leave Balance

Employees can check their current leave balance through the enterprise HR system.

The leave balance includes available annual leave and other applicable leave categories assigned to the employee.

## Leave Request

Employees should submit leave requests through the approved HR process.

A leave request should include the requested start date, end date, and leave type.

## Approval

Leave requests may require manager approval according to the organization's leave rules.

## Helpdesk

For questions about leave balances or leave requests, employees may contact the HR helpdesk.

The AI assistant must not invent leave balances. If a current balance is not available from an authorized enterprise system, the assistant should state that it cannot retrieve the current balance.
