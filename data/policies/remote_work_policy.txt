# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees may work remotely when permitted by their role, team requirements, and applicable company policy.

## Equipment

Employees working remotely are responsible for using approved company equipment and following applicable security requirements.

## Security

Employees must follow company security policies when accessing enterprise systems remotely.

Sensitive company information should only be accessed through approved systems and connections.

## Helpdesk

Technical problems with approved remote-work equipment or access should be reported through the enterprise IT helpdesk.
