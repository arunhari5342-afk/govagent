# IT Helpdesk Policy

## Support Requests

Employees can create IT support tickets for approved technical issues.

A support ticket should contain a clear description of the problem and relevant information needed by the helpdesk.

## Ticket Priority

Normal issues should use the standard support priority unless the organization's support rules specify otherwise.

Critical incidents should follow the organization's incident escalation process.

## Ticket Status

Employees can use the approved helpdesk system to check the status of their support tickets.

## AI Assistant

The AI assistant may help employees understand helpdesk procedures.

Creating a ticket is an external action and must be performed only through an approved and authorized tool.
